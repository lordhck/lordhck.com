Optical data transfer

Have you ever transferred data through a camera?

Not scanned a QR code to open a website, but actually transferred files using nothing but light?

Well, this started as one of those slightly questionable ideas you get while sitting at a bar.

The idea was simple: take a file, split it into smaller chunks, turn each chunk into a QR code and flash them one after another on a screen. Point a camera at it, collect the data and put the file back together.

It should work, right?

Turns out, it does.

About 20 minutes later, we had ourselves a working Proof of Concept.

No Bluetooth, no Wi-Fi, no cables. Just a screen, a camera and a whole lot of QR codes flashing back and forth.

You can try it yourself:

https://lordhck.github.io/qrcode

Sometimes the dumbest ideas are the most fun to build.
