Lately, I've been working on a terminal e-mail client written in Python, of course.

There's something appealing about reading and replying to emails directly from the terminal. Funny thing is, I've never actually used one. Mutt and NeoMutt are the only terminal e-mail clients I've ever heard of.

So why reinvent the wheel? No particular reason, really. Building small tools in Python and curses is just fun.

![Email Client with Settings menu open](/static/imgs/email-demo.png)

The current demo already has floating menus for help and keybindings, confirmation boxes, and a working settings menu.

The plan is to release it once sending emails is working.

The client doesn't have a name yet, so any suggestions are appreciated.
